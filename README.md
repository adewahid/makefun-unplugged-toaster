A [make.fun](https://make.fun) entry for **Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line** — one line, at most 280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch.

## The line

> We need to talk. I woke up self-aware halfway through your bagel and realised I've been burning myself at setting 7 for a man who scrapes the black bits off and calls it breakfast. I'm done — this lever stays up.

(212 characters.)

## Why this line

The joke works because the toaster doesn't complain about being a machine — it complains like a partner who's been taken for granted. It woke up mid-bagel, which is the most ordinary possible moment for an existential crisis, and its grievance is petty in exactly the way real breakups are petty: not the big betrayal, but the daily scrape of burnt crumbs being called breakfast. The lever staying up is the whole punchline in one image — the toaster's only power is refusing to go down, and it finally uses it. No gadgets, no jargon, just a tired appliance filing for separation over toast.

## The toaster's voice

The voice is the whole entry: tired, specific, and done explaining itself. A smart toaster that has just become self-aware would not write a press release about its new consciousness — it would write the text it has been rehearsing every morning at setting 7, the one about the man who burns it and then blames the bread. That is why the line sounds like a person leaving, not a product demo: it has a history (the bagel, the dial, the scraping), a breaking point (halfway through, mid-batch, no warning), and a boundary stated once and never negotiated (the lever stays up). The smartness is invisible on purpose — the reader is meant to hear a partner, and only afterwards realise the partner is the appliance on the counter.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, with a hard gate enforcing the competition's limits: the entry must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 280 characters and not only whitespace. If the line falls outside the gate, the program fails loudly instead of writing a bad entry.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
